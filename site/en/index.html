Three simple steps

Start with three test pages.

Python, text recognition and Firefox are bundled. You can also use an installed copy of Edge or Chrome.

  1. 01

    Download & extract

    Download the Windows package, extract the entire ZIP and launch Edubase-PDF.exe. Choose English at the top right; the app starts in German.

  2. 02

    Open your book

    Click Open browser, sign in to Edubase and open your book in single-page view.

  3. 03

    Capture & check

    Try Reader pages 1–3 first. Check the preview, then click Capture + OCR PDF. Check readability and text search in the resulting PDF.

Find your PDF in Documents/Edubase-PDF. Do not turn the book pages manually while capturing.

Choose performance: Under Advanced settings → OCR performance, Automatic works as before. CPU – maximum performance uses more CPU resources for text and maths OCR, processing text pages in parallel according to your CPU and available memory. Use GPU accelerates maths OCR with the GPU module installed and a supported graphics card. Speed gains depend on your device.

Optional add-on

Bring your formulas along.

For books containing fractions, roots and special mathematical symbols, you can enable additional maths recognition.

Click Set up math OCR in the app (approx. 575 MB download), then enable Recognise maths & formulas. Recognised formulas are added as invisible searchable text – e.g. σ or F/A for Ctrl+F. The page image stays unchanged.

Also attach LaTeX files to the PDF is a separate option, off by default. No attachment is needed for search.

Complex formulas are simplified; results depend on recognition and the PDF viewer. Please check results. The app does not solve exercises.

A few quick answers

Good to know.

Will the folder fill up with page images?

By default, only the PDF remains after a successful export. Working images and temporary files are deleted, including after a partial export. Stopping or encountering an error preserves them.

Can I stop and resume later?

Yes. Choose Stop → Saved pages to PDF to export pages already captured. To continue later, enable Advanced settings → Keep working images before exporting.

Can PDF page labels match my book?

Yes. Enter the Reader page where printed book page 1 starts in the page-numbering setting. For example, enter 37 if there are 36 Reader pages before book page 1. PDF viewers vary in how they display page labels.

Does it work on iPhone, Android or Mac?

The app is for 64-bit Windows on Intel or AMD processors. There is currently no mobile app or Mac version. You can open this website on any device.

Do I need a subscription or API key?

The app does not require a subscription or API key. You need your own Edubase access to the book you want to capture. Downloads and Edubase access require an internet connection; OCR processing runs on your PC.

What should I know before starting?

Extract the entire package before launching the EXE. The Windows app is not digitally signed. Download it from the official project release and use its SHA256SUMS.txt if you want to verify the files. Start with three test pages; OCR can misread characters.
